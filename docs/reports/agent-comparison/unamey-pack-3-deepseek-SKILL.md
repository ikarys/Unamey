## Brief

A seed bank run by a regional agricultural institute. Four curators keep about 3,000 seed lots of local crop varieties in freezers, test the germination of each lot every few years, and must decide which lots to regenerate in the field before they lose viability.

Reference screen: Regional Seed Bank is a desktop collection-management tool for a regional agricultural institute's four curators, who preserve roughly 3,000 seed lots of local crop varieties in freezers. On the main Viability Dashboard they first scan lots whose germination has fallen below threshold, tests that are due or overdue, and regeneration candidates before those lots lose viability. The screen needs to combine freezer inventory, recent germination results, viability trends, and a prioritized regeneration queue with clear status and due dates. Curators can open a lot, schedule or log a germination test, assign a field plot for regeneration, and update freezer locations. It is a serious, data-dense work tool used at a desk during regular collection reviews and seasonal regeneration planning.

Users: Four seed-bank curators at a regional agricultural institute, working from desktop workstations in a lab office during focused daily collection reviews and seasonal regeneration planning.
Data objects: Seed lot, Crop variety, Germination test, Viability reading, Regeneration plan, Freezer, Field plot

## Direction

**Archetype: Analytics board.** Charts lead, chrome recedes: a KPI band with big tabular numerals, then two or three large charts. Muted neutrals, one accent, a disciplined 3-tone chart palette.

Dashboard is the right structure because the curators’ first job is monitoring and comparing viability, due tests and regeneration need over time, not moving cards or editing a grid. A compact top bar keeps collection date-range, freezer filter and search; the KPI band gives tabular counts for lots below threshold, tests due/overdue, regen candidates and freezer occupancy, each with a sparkline. Two large inline-SVG charts show viability trends by crop variety and recent germination results against threshold, a narrow capacity panel lists freezer zones and field plots, and the ranked regeneration queue table carries seed lot, variety, status, due date and row actions to open a lot, log a test or assign a plot.

Structure: Compact topbar with a date-range control, KPI band of 4-6 tiles with sparklines, then a 2/3 + 1/3 chart row, then a table or ranked list. No left sidebar, or a collapsed icon rail.

## Shell

The shell of `reference.html`: No sidebar. Two-row sticky top bar: row one is brand 'Regional Seed Bank' + collection context, search field, notification bell with overdue count, curator avatars; row two is the page title 'Viability Dashboard' with review date, then date-range select, freezer select and the primary 'Schedule test' button. Keep it on every screen with the active item marked; only the content region changes from page to page.

Reference structure: **Dashboard.** A date-range control in the header, a band of KPI tiles, a row of charts, then a ranked list or table; read-only, no editing. One screen, no sidebar needed.

## Do

- numbers 28-40px, tabular, with unit and delta
- charts drawn as inline SVG with axis labels and units
- accent + two neutral tones for series, red/green only for deltas
- legend inline, not a floating box

## Don't

- no 3D or donut-heavy dashboards
- no card per tiny metric in a 6-column grid
- no rainbow series palette
- no gauge decorations
- No indigo/violet accents, no white + slate neutrals, no Inter or Roboto: that is the generic look this pack exists to avoid.
