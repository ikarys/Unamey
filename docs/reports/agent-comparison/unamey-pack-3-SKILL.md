## Brief

A seed bank run by a regional agricultural institute. Four curators keep about 3,000 seed lots of local crop varieties in freezers, test the germination of each lot every few years, and must decide which lots to regenerate in the field before they lose viability.

Reference screen: Curators at a regional agricultural institute open the console to review the condition of thousands of stored seed lots. The main screen shows the most urgent viability risks, recent germination results, and which lots need field regeneration soon. They filter by crop, freezer, risk level, and test age to find lots that require action. From there they can open a lot, record a germination test, assign a regeneration task, and check storage locations. The goal is to keep the collection healthy and avoid losing local crop varieties before they are re-grown.

Users: Four curators at a regional agricultural institute use it on desktop workstations for daily triage and weekly planning sessions.
Data objects: SeedLot, CropVariety, GerminationTest, FreezerLocation, RegenerationTask, ViabilityAlert

## Direction

**Archetype: Dense operator console.** Linear / Datadog energy: a tool for people who live in it eight hours a day. Dark, compact, keyboard-first, numbers in monospace, every pixel earns its place.

Use master-detail because curators triage seed lots one at a time: the dominant left list shows urgent SeedLot rows with dot-and-text ViabilityAlert status, CropVariety names, monospace latest GerminationTest age, FreezerLocation code, and RegenerationTask due status, under inline crop, freezer, risk, and test-age filters. The right inspector gives the selected lot’s full viability profile, storage location, recent germination results, and 48px-tall persistent actions to record a germination test, assign a regeneration task, or check storage, with keyboard shortcut labels shown next to actions. A top bar keeps a command-palette hint and high-contrast status, while list rows and action buttons remain 48px tall with 14px minimum text and no hover-only affordances.

Structure: Icon rail or narrow sidebar, a command palette hint in the topbar, one dominant data region (table, status board or log), inspector panel on the right. Dense 32px rows, sticky headers, filters inline.

## Shell

The shell of `reference.html`: 48px topbar with brand left, command-palette hint center, system status dots right; no side navigation rail. Keep it on every screen with the active item marked; only the content region changes from page to page.

Reference structure: **Master list + detail.** A narrow list or inbox on the left (30-35% width, one row per object with status and age), the selected object's full detail on the right with its actions in a sticky header. No dashboard elements.

## Do

- tabular numerals everywhere
- keyboard shortcuts shown next to actions
- status as coloured dot + text
- monospace for ids, timestamps and values

## Don't

- no cards inside cards
- no large hero numbers
- no decorative gradients
- no rounded pill buttons
- No indigo/violet accents, no white + slate neutrals, no Inter or Roboto: that is the generic look this pack exists to avoid.
