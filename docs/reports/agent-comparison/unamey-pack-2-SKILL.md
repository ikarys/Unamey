## Brief

An internal support desk for a B2B SaaS company. Eight agents handle customer tickets all day at their desks.

Reference screen: Eight B2B SaaS support agents open this internal desk at the start of each work session to triage incoming customer tickets. The main screen shows a prioritized ticket queue with customer context, SLA timers, and conversation previews so agents can quickly decide what to claim next. Agents need to see ticket status, priority, owner, response deadline, and related customer history without leaving the view. Key actions are claiming a ticket, replying, tagging issues, assigning teammates, escalating blocked cases, and resolving conversations. The product supports steady, keyboard-driven work across a full day of customer support.

Users: Support agents and team leads use it at office desks during business hours to monitor, respond to, and resolve customer tickets.
Data objects: Ticket, Customer, Conversation, Agent, SLA, Tag, Macro

## Direction

**Archetype: Dense operator console.** Linear / Datadog energy: a tool for people who live in it eight hours a day. Dark, compact, keyboard-first, numbers in monospace, every pixel earns its place.

Master-detail fits this desk because agents process tickets one at a time from a prioritized queue and need the selected ticket’s full context beside the list. The dominant left region is a dense ticket queue where each row shows ticket id, status dot, priority, owner, SLA countdown, tags, and a one-line conversation preview. The right inspector supplies customer history, selected conversation, SLA deadline, tag and macro pickers, agent assignment, and claim, reply, escalate, and resolve actions.

Structure: Icon rail or narrow sidebar, a command palette hint in the topbar, one dominant data region (table, status board or log), inspector panel on the right. Dense 32px rows, sticky headers, filters inline.

## Shell

The shell of `reference.html`: 56px icon rail (left) with 5 nav icons (Inbox, Agents, Reports, Macros, Settings) + 48px topbar with brand mark, search field with ⌘K hint, date range, and right-side agent avatar. Keep it on every screen with the active item marked; only the content region changes from page to page.

Reference structure: **Master list + detail.** A narrow list or inbox on the left (30-35% width, one row per object with status and age), the selected object's full detail on the right with its actions in a sticky header. No dashboard elements.

## Do

- tabular numerals everywhere
- keyboard shortcuts shown next to actions
- status as coloured dot + text
- monospace for ids, timestamps and values

## Don't

- no cards inside cards
- no large hero numbers
- no decorative gradients
- no rounded pill buttons
- No indigo/violet accents, no white + slate neutrals, no Inter or Roboto: that is the generic look this pack exists to avoid.
