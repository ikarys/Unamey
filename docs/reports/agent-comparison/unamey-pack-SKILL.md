## Brief

An internal tool for the 8 support agents of a B2B SaaS company who handle customer tickets all day at a desk. The main screen is the agents' working screen: the queue of open tickets sorted by SLA deadline (customer, subject, priority, time left, assignee), the selected ticket with its full conversation and the customer's details (plan, open tickets, account owner), and the actions an agent uses every minute: reply, assign to a colleague, change priority, merge with another ticket, close. Agents switch between "mine", "unassigned" and "all" queues and search by customer.

Reference screen: Ticket Desk is an internal support console for a B2B SaaS team. Eight support agents open it at their desks and start by scanning the open ticket queue ordered by SLA deadline. They need to see customer, subject, priority, time left, and assignee for each ticket, then open the selected ticket to read the full conversation and customer account context. The main actions are reply, assign to a colleague, change priority, merge with another ticket, and close. Agents can switch between mine, unassigned, and all queues and search by customer.

Users: Eight B2B SaaS support agents use it daily at their desks for long, high-volume ticket-handling sessions.
Data objects: Ticket, Conversation, Customer, Agent, Priority, Plan, Account

## Direction

**Archetype: Dense operator console.** Linear / Datadog energy: a tool for people who live in it eight hours a day. Dark, compact, keyboard-first, numbers in monospace, every pixel earns its place.

Master-detail puts the open-ticket queue in the left master pane, one compact row per ticket with customer, subject, priority, time left, and assignee, while mine/unassigned/all filters and customer search sit in a thin toolbar. The right detail pane shows the selected ticket’s full conversation, a customer account strip with plan, account owner, and open-ticket count, and a sticky action header for reply, assign, change priority, merge, and close.

Structure: Icon rail or narrow sidebar, a command palette hint in the topbar, one dominant data region (table, status board or log), inspector panel on the right. Dense 32px rows, sticky headers, filters inline.

## Shell

The shell of `reference.html`: 56px icon rail (far left, 4 nav icons: Queue active, Customers, Reports, Settings) + 44px topbar (left: app name 'Ticket Desk', center: command palette hint ⌘K, right: agent avatar 'SC' + status dot). Keep it on every screen with the active item marked; only the content region changes from page to page.

Reference structure: **Master list + detail.** A narrow list or inbox on the left (30-35% width, one row per object with status and age), the selected object's full detail on the right with its actions in a sticky header. No dashboard elements.

## Do

- tabular numerals everywhere
- keyboard shortcuts shown next to actions
- status as coloured dot + text
- monospace for ids, timestamps and values

## Don't

- no cards inside cards
- no large hero numbers
- no decorative gradients
- no rounded pill buttons
- No indigo/violet accents, no white + slate neutrals, no Inter or Roboto: that is the generic look this pack exists to avoid.
