Desktop application rules (binding: the brief says the product is an installed desktop app, not a website).
The body IS the app window: fill the viewport (html, body height 100%, margin 0), design for a 1200x760 window. No page around it, no floating window with a shadow, no browser-site patterns (hero, site header with links, footer, breadcrumbs, cookie bar).
Draw the window chrome the app owns (custom title bar, as with Tauri or Electron without native decorations), following the OS the brief names; Windows 11 when it names none.
Windows 11: title bar 32px, or 40px when tabs live in it; caption buttons (minimize, maximize, close) flush top right, each 46px wide and full title-bar height, thin 10px line glyphs in the text colour; on hover the close button turns #c42b1c with a white glyph, the other two take a 6% text-colour tint. Caption buttons are never accent-coloured, outlined, rounded or boxed. Font "Segoe UI Variable", "Segoe UI", system-ui; UI text 14px, captions 12px. Radius 4px on controls, 8px on menus and flyouts. Controls 32px tall.
macOS: traffic lights (12px circles, 8px apart, #ff5f57 #febc2e #28c840) 12px from the top-left; title bar 28px, or a unified toolbar 52px. Font -apple-system, 13px. Radius 6px controls, 10px popovers.
Linux (GNOME): header bar 46px, round close button on the right, Cantarell or system-ui.
Tabs in the title bar (Windows Terminal, Notepad, Edge): the selected tab has exactly the content surface colour and merges into it (top corners 8px, no bottom edge, no outline, no coloured stroke or top border); unselected tabs are muted text straight on the title bar, separated by a 1px divider at most; the tab close glyph is 16px, shown on the selected and hovered tab. The "+" sits right after the last tab, not at the far end.
Colour: chrome and surfaces are neutral greys like the OS (Windows dark: title bar #202020, content #272727; light: title bar #f3f3f3, content #fbfbfb). Any tint stays under 3% chroma: no navy, purple or brand-hued backgrounds. The accent marks only a few things: caret, selection, focus ring, checked checkbox, primary button, the active item of a list. One accent; never a saturated accent against a saturated tinted background.
Show the rest state: no element focused, no focus ring, no hovered control, unless the page is about that state.
Borders 1px at 6-10% text colour; shadows only on menus and dialogs. Scrollbars thin and overlay (no permanent gutter). Status bar, when useful, 24px, 12px muted text.
Shortcuts appear as plain text in menus and tooltips (Ctrl+N), never as keycap chips in the title bar.
Keep the archetype for typography details, density and tone inside the content area; the OS conventions above win for the window chrome.
