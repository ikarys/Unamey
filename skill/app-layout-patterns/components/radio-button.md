## circular-radio-button — Circular radio button
- Looks: 16-20px circular control; unchecked is hollow, checked has a centered filled dot; can use beveled or themed surface treatment when matching a 3D app style.
- Use when: Single-choice selection among a small set of mutually exclusive options.
- Avoid when: Multi-select, long option lists, or dense forms where checkboxes, segmented controls, or dropdowns fit better.
- Build notes: Support checked, unchecked, focus-visible, and disabled states; use arrow-key navigation within a radio group; keep hit area at least 24px.
