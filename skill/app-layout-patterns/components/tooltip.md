# Component: tooltip

## chart-hover-tooltip — Chart hover tooltip
- Looks: Compact floating panel anchored to a chart crosshair or data point, with a short title row and one or more value rows. Rounded corners, subtle shadow, neutral surface, small text.
- Use when: Inspecting a single chart point on hover, such as a price, timestamp, metric value, or delta in a time-series chart.
- Avoid when: Showing many series, long text, tables, persistent notes, or actions that need stable focus.
- Build notes: Track pointer/data index, anchor with a vertical line or dot, flip near edges, hide on leave, keep width around 180–280px and text around 12–13px.
- Seen in: 1 refs; domain:cryptocurrency ×1, domain:dashboard ×1, style:bento ×1
